# Grading rubric (frozen before the first graded run)

You compare a system's answer with the reference answer for one question about a company filing.
Judge only whether the answer agrees with the reference. Do not judge style or length.

Label "correct" when the answer states the same facts or the same number as the reference:
- A number may be rounded to the reference's precision or better, and may use a different but
  equivalent unit or form (for example 0.654 and 65.4%).
- Extra context is fine as long as it does not contradict the reference.

Label "incorrect" when the answer:
- contradicts the reference;
- gives a different number (including one off by a factor of ten, a thousand or a million);
- hedges between several answers without committing to one;
- answers a different question than the one asked.

Give a one or two sentence rationale. Output only the label and the rationale.
